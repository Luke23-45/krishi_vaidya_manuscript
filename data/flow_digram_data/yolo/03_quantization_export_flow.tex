  \textbf{Edge Optimization}\;\textcolor{muted}{\textemdash}\;

  (usually by 4x) and speeds up inference on mobile CPUs/NPUs. The validation
  set is used during calibration to compute the scaling factors, minimizing the
  inevitable drop in accuracy.};
